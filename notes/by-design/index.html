NOTE18 NOVEMBER 2026

3 out of 4 trades lose.That's the strategy, not a bug.

TrustAlgo is a trend-following system. Most of its trades close at a loss, on purpose. This note explains why, what that looks like in numbers, and why we froze the strategy instead of tuning it.

How the edge works

The system enters when a move starts on BTC, ETH or SOL. Most moves fail quickly. When that happens, a stop closes the position at a loss that was fixed before entry.

When a move turns into a trend, the position stays open and the gain is many times the loss. A handful of trends each year pay for all the stops.

About half the months are negative. A run of 15–20 stops in a row is normal behaviour, not a malfunction.

Why we froze it

A strategy like this is easiest to break by hand. After ten stops in a row the instinct is to change something, and the change usually removes the very trades that would have paid for the losses. So version 1.0.9 is frozen:

  • No parameter changes until the annual review in November 2027.
  • Any change must hold on a year of data it was never fitted to.
  • Drawdown limits are set in advance: review, then halve exposure, then stop new entries.

What this means for you

If you run TrustAlgo, you will see long stretches of small losses between larger wins. That is the system working as designed.

Your funds stay on your Hyperliquid account. The API wallet can trade, it cannot withdraw, and you can revoke it at any time.
