Trends pay. Ranges wait.v1.1 trades the ranges too.

Version 1.1 went live on 24 November 2026. Every trend-following rule stays exactly as it was. What’s new are range models on BTC and ETH, built for the stretches when there is no trend to follow.

Why range models

Trend following earns its year in a few strong moves. Between them it is often flat, waiting. Range models work in the opposite conditions: they look for crowded positioning in a sideways market and trade the return toward balance.

In our tests the daily returns of the two families were essentially uncorrelated. That is why they belong on the same account.

How they share the account

  • A range position opens only when the trend model on that coin is flat.
  • When the trend model enters, the range position on that coin is closed first. Trend following never loses a trade because of it: in the backtest its 1,210 trades are identical with and without range models.
  • SOL stays trend-only. No range model on SOL passed our tests.

How they execute

  • Entries and exits use post-only limit orders (maker fees) for up to 2 hours, then market.
  • Every position gets a stop on Hyperliquid within 30 seconds of the fill.
  • Same non-custodial setup: same API wallet, no new permissions. Funds never leave your account.
