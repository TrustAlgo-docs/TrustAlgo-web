How the fee is paid

At the end of each quarter (1 January, 1 April, 1 July, 1 October) TrustAlgo checks your account. A fee is due only if your balance ended above your high-water mark: 20% of the new profit, net of your deposits, withdrawals and transfers. If there is no new profit, there is nothing to pay.

The fee is paid in USDC on Arbitrum One, from your wallet, not from your Hyperliquid balance. You choose one of two methods after approval, and you can switch at any time with /billing.

A

Automatic (smart contract)

You sign an approval for the TrustAlgo billing contract on Arbitrum One. Signing moves no funds; at the end of a profitable quarter the contract collects the fee due.

Keep enough USDC in your wallet on Arbitrum at quarter end, plus a little ETH. If your USDC is all on Hyperliquid, withdraw the amount to your wallet first.

B

Manual

You receive the invoice in the Telegram terminal. Within 72 hours, send the exact amount of USDC on Arbitrum One to the address shown in the invoice, then confirm with /settle <transaction hash>.

Not enough funds for the smart contract? You can still pay manually within 72 hours, as in method B.

Until the fee is paid, no new positions are opened; positions already open keep being managed.

If it is not paid within 72 hours, the account is deactivated and TrustAlgo stops trading it. Any position still open stays on your Hyperliquid account, for you to manage.

Rules while the model is running

  • Use a wallet dedicated to TrustAlgo. It keeps results, fees and positions clean.
  • Do not trade BTC, ETH or SOL by hand on the same account. The model would treat your positions as its own.
  • Do not change leverage or margin mode on BTC, ETH or SOL. The model sets them before each trade.
  • Deposits and withdrawals are fine and never count as profit. Avoid withdrawing most of the balance while positions are open: they need margin.
  • Keep at least 1,000 USDC on the account, or orders can fall below Hyperliquid’s minimum sizes.
  • To change the split between coins, use /reallocate. Do not create a new API wallet for it.

Renewing the API key

Hyperliquid API wallets last at most 180 days. The terminal reminds you 7 days and 24 hours before expiry. To renew:

  1. On app.hyperliquid.xyz/API create a new API wallet, copy its private key and authorize it with Days Valid set to MAX.
  2. In the Telegram terminal send /renew: it asks for your main wallet address and the new key, and deletes your messages after reading them.

Nothing else changes: balance, high-water mark and allocation stay as they are.

How to stop

  1. Ask to pause with /help in the Telegram terminal. No new positions are opened from then on.
  2. Let open positions close on their own, or close them yourself on Hyperliquid.
  3. Revoke the API wallet on app.hyperliquid.xyz/API.
  4. Withdraw whenever you like: only your main wallet can, and Hyperliquid charges 1 USDC per withdrawal.

Invoices already issued must still be paid.
