05 — SECURITY

What you authorize, and what happens to the key.

You authorize an API wallet, not your main wallet. Its key is never stored in plaintext. Here is what happens to it, step by step, from the moment you submit it.

1. TRANSMISSION

The key travels over an encrypted HTTPS/TLS connection directly to the isolated onboarding service.

2. ENCRYPTION

As soon as it is validated, the key is encrypted in memory with authenticated symmetric encryption. It is never written to logs.

3. STORAGE

The database stores only the encrypted key. The master key needed to decrypt it is kept separately, outside the database.

4. EXECUTION

The key is decrypted in memory only by the process that sends orders to Hyperliquid, a moment before signing, and discarded right after.

YOU KEEP

Custody and control

  • Your main wallet and withdrawal rights
  • Your USDC, on your Hyperliquid account
  • The right to revoke the API wallet, at any time
TRUSTALGO DOES

  • Places a stop on every position

Touch your funds

  • Trade on any venue other than Hyperliquid
  • See or use your main wallet’s key

06 — FAQ

Questions before you start.

How much do I need to start?

At least 1,000 USDC on your Hyperliquid account. Smaller balances run into Hyperliquid’s minimum order sizes on BTC, ETH and SOL.

What does it cost?

20% of new profits, calculated each quarter above your high-water mark: if your account is not above its previous peak, you pay nothing. Deposits and withdrawals are excluded from the calculation. There are no setup or management fees. Fees are paid in USDC, either through a smart-contract approval on Arbitrum or manually from the Telegram terminal.

Can I withdraw or stop at any time?

Yes. Your funds stay on your Hyperliquid account and only your main wallet can withdraw them. To stop, ask on Telegram to pause new trades, or revoke the API wallet on app.hyperliquid.xyz/API. If you revoke it, any open position stays on your account and is yours to manage.

What losses should I expect?

Losses are part of the design. Most trades close at a small, predefined loss, and long losing streaks are normal. In backtests since 2021 the deepest drawdown of the full portfolio was about 27%, and stress tests show it can be much deeper. Only allocate capital you can afford to see fall. What to expect, in numbers →

How much does each trade risk?

Position size is set by risk, not by conviction: each position risks roughly 1.5–2% of the capital allocated to that coin, defined by its stop. The default split is 50% BTC, 25% ETH, 25% SOL, the allocation the model was tested with. You can change it.

What will I see after joining?

A private Telegram terminal with your balance, open positions, trade history, a weekly report and your quarterly statement. It is available in English, Italian, Spanish and French.

Will other models be added?

Yes, but only models that pass the same process: years of backtests, a frozen version and monthly live audits. Each model’s live results will be published separately.

Is my API key safe?

The API wallet can trade but cannot withdraw, and its key is encrypted on arrival and never stored in plaintext. How the key is handled →
