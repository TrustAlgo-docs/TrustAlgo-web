No experience needed. This guide takes you from zero to a funded Hyperliquid account connected to TrustAlgo. You do it once; most of the time is spent waiting for transfers.

What you need

  • A computer (easiest) or a smartphone
  • About 1,000 USDC, a digital dollar you can buy with euros or dollars
  • A few euros of ETH to pay the network fee of the deposit
  • A Telegram account (step 05)
  • Pen and paper

Six words to know

WALLETAn app that holds your crypto. You own it, and nobody else can open it, TrustAlgo included.
RECOVERY PHRASE12 or 24 words that restore your wallet. Whoever has them owns your money. Paper only, never shared.
USDCA digital dollar: 1 USDC is worth about 1 US dollar. It is what you deposit on Hyperliquid.
ARBITRUMThe network your USDC travels on to reach Hyperliquid. When an exchange asks for a network, choose Arbitrum One.
GASThe small network fee for moving funds, paid in ETH on Arbitrum: a few cents per transfer. Keep a few euros of ETH in your wallet. Trading on Hyperliquid itself has no gas.
API WALLETA second key that can trade on your Hyperliquid account but can never withdraw. The only key you give TrustAlgo.

Step by step

01

Create your wallet10 MIN

Install Rabby (rabby.io) or MetaMask (metamask.io) in your browser, only from its official website. Choose Create new wallet and set a password.

The app shows your recovery phrase: write the words on paper, in order, and keep the paper somewhere safe. Never photograph it, never type it into a website.

Your wallet now has an address starting with 0x, 42 characters long. This is your main wallet address: it is safe to share, and you will paste it in the TrustAlgo form.

02

Get USDC on Arbitrum15–30 MIN

On an exchange you can use in your country (for example Coinbase, Kraken or Binance), buy the USDC you want to use, at least 1,000 USDC, plus a few euros of ETH (5–10 € is plenty). The ETH is needed: it pays the network fee (“gas”) of the deposit to Hyperliquid, usually a few cents. Without ETH on Arbitrum the deposit cannot be sent.

Withdraw both to your wallet address. For both, when the exchange asks for the network, choose Arbitrum One. If you see both USDC and USDC.e, choose USDC.

Send a small test amount first, check that it arrives in your wallet, then send the rest.

03

Deposit on Hyperliquid5 MIN

Type app.hyperliquid.xyz in your browser and bookmark it. Click Connect, choose your wallet and approve. Connecting is free and moves no money.

Click Deposit, choose USDC from Arbitrum, enter the amount and confirm in your wallet; it may ask you to confirm twice, and each confirmation spends a few cents of ETH. The balance appears on Hyperliquid in about a minute. From then on, trading on Hyperliquid needs no ETH.

Never deposit less than 5 USDC: smaller deposits are not credited and are lost.

04

Create the API wallet5 MIN

On Hyperliquid open More → API (app.hyperliquid.xyz/API). Type a name, for example trustalgo, and click Generate.

A private key appears: 64 characters, usually starting with 0x. Copy it now, it is shown only once. Then click Authorize API Wallet, set Days Valid to MAX (180 days), click Authorize and confirm in your wallet.

This key can trade on your account but can never withdraw. It is the only key you give TrustAlgo. More detail in the setup guide →

05

Install Telegram5 MIN

TrustAlgo’s client terminal runs on Telegram. Install it from the App Store, Google Play or telegram.org and create an account with your phone number. The terminal is available in English, Italian, Spanish and French.

06

Submit and activate5 MIN

Open the Get started form on trustalgo.co and fill in your name, your main wallet address (step 01) and the API wallet private key (step 04). Leave the allocation at 50 / 25 / 25 unless you have a reason to change it.

Submit: Telegram opens, press Start. Your account is approved within 24 hours, and you receive a message with your starting balance and how fees are paid. The first trade arrives when the model signals; it can take days.

Safety rules

Your recovery phrase never leaves the paper. TrustAlgo will never ask for it, and neither will Hyperliquid.

The only key you give TrustAlgo is the API wallet key. It can trade, it cannot withdraw, and you can revoke it at any time on app.hyperliquid.xyz/API.

TrustAlgo never messages you first to ask for funds or keys. Support answers only inside the Telegram terminal.

Type trustalgo.co and app.hyperliquid.xyz yourself. Beware of look-alike sites and sponsored search results. Official channels →

What it costs to set up

Exchange fees to buy USDC (set by your exchange), a few euros of ETH for Arbitrum network fees (most of it stays in your wallet for next time), and nothing on Hyperliquid’s side for depositing. Withdrawing from Hyperliquid later costs 1 USDC. TrustAlgo charges nothing to join; its only fee is 20% of new profits, settled quarterly.
